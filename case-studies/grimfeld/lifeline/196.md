## What to build

A kit **Date picker button**: an icon-only calendar button with a required aria-label. Picking a day acts at once and closes the popover. It takes `min`/`max` like the Date picker.

It replaces:
- the Journal's "Jump to date"
- the Calendar surface's "Jump to date". On the phone it moves into the Calendar's Phone dock (ADR 0064) and is shown there; today it is hidden below `md`.
- Parts' "Read on another day" (capped at today): picking a day marks the Part read on that day.

All work lands on the integration branch **`date-picker`** (off `origin/master`); PRs target it, not master. Only #201 merges it to master, so master never shows two kinds of date control.

## PR #209 Pick every date from the kit's calendar, not the browser's

Every date in Lifeline is now picked from the kit's calendar instead of the browser's native picker, on phone and desk alike (ADR 0087). Spec: #194.

## What changes for the user
- One calendar everywhere (shadcn Calendar on react-day-picker 10), in a popover from a button showing the formatted date. Weeks start on Monday, names follow the browser locale, the header has month/year dropdowns (back to 1500 where historical dates make sense), and there's a "Today" shortcut, "Clear" on optional dates, and days outside min/max disabled.
- Date-and-time fields (Swearjar, Lifelevel, Stats, the editor's datetime Field) are a date picker next to the native time input. Picking a day with no time yet fills in now, capped at `max`; a time that's already set is kept.
- Tasks' Move dialog shows the calendar inline: tap a day to move the Task.
- Jump-to-date (Journal, Calendar surface) and Parts' "Read on another day" are calendar icon buttons. The Calendar's now shows on the phone too, in its Phone dock.
- Plain time inputs stay native.
