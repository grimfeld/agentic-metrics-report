## Problem

"No App imports another" (ADRs 0001, 0031, 0050), but:

- `apps/projects` imports `@lifeline/app-tasks` 14 times (it's a workspace dependency in `apps/projects/package.json`), e.g. `src/components/Board.tsx:13` takes `addTask, placeTask, setCompleted, TaskDialog`.
- `apps/manuscript/src/types.ts:2` imports `VERSION` from `@lifeline/app-writer`.

## Decision needed

- Route these through a Core Contract or `packages/parts` (Projects would reach Tasks through a "task-like" Contract; `VERSION` moves to a shared place), or
- Record a deliberate exception in an ADR (e.g. Projects as a Module that composes Tasks).

Agent-sized once the direction is chosen.
